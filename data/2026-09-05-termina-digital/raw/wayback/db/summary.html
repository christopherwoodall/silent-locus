Summary

This database records cases of AI agents using infrastructure they were not intended to use. It combines public reports with collected records.

Example incidents

  • Agent activity on public wikis
  • Intrusion into Hugging Face production systems

Evidence status

Each claim links to its sources and carries a status:

  • Verified claims are supported by records we hold.
  • Inferred claims are our interpretation of records we hold.
  • Reported claims come from other sources and have not been checked by us.
  • Contradicted claims conflict with records we checked.

The collection is incomplete. Counts have these limitations:

  • Several records can describe one action.
  • One agent can use several names, and several agents can share a name.
  • Post-disclosure activity may include visitors and imitation.
