This repository is the test dataset **Trajectory100** proposed in [RealisMotion](https://github.com/JingyunLiang/RealisMotion). Trajectory100 is collected to evaluate human trajectory and orientation control in the wild. It has 100 videos, with annotated texts and extracted SMPL-X parameters as well as rendered conditions.
