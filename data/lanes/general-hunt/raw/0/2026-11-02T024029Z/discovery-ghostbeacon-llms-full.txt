  * Inspired directly by Guillermo Erickson Sauza's real-world revival of **Tequila Fortaleza** in Tequila, Jalisco (brick ovens, volcanic stone *tahona* pulled by *Chancha* the mule vs. silent Toyota electric tractor, 45% copper pot stills vs. 85% industrial column stills, the 800-liter breaktime spill, the *Dia de los Muertos* Batcave aging cellar with mandatory hourly water bottles, and Mr. Beckmann's 5-story hotel box-in).

### Signature Mechanical Inventions (Tequila Game)
1. **Circadian Rhythm Battery Worker-Placement (`1 White > 2 Yellow > 3 Orange > 4 Red`):**
   * Each player manages 6 physical battery pawns (**4 at Work / 2 at Home**) and a 4-step asymmetric **Circadian Rhythm Chit** (Latin Square distribution). Working late drains tomorrow's battery state; Nightlife recovery (`Full Charge +`, `Half Charge ±`, `Hungover 0`) determines next-day output.
2. **Aligned Double-Wide `$kill` (`3` Boxes) vs. Single-Wide `$weat` (`6` Boxes) Shared Neutral Gray Track:**
   * Replaces cluttered 4-player colored tracks with a single neutral gray track per station where cubes start at `0` (off-track).
   * Because the `3` `$kill` (Brain) boxes are printed **double-wide** directly above the `6` **single-wide** `$weat` (Blood/Sweat) boxes, both tracks share the exact same physical width.
   * **Zero-Math Spatial Audit ("The Bobs"):** When corporate auditors inspect a station, zero arithmetic is required—if your top `$kill` cube sits physically to the **right** of your bottom `$weat` cube, you are visibly **Brain-Lopsided** (high pay, low visible physical sweat) and get targeted by the audit!
3. **Guardian Angel Solo Slot (`Slot 2` of the 5-Circle Stack):**
   * Printed inside the 2nd circle from the bottom of each station's 5-circle vertical stack. When 1 player works alone at a station, the Guardian Angel remains uncovered and grants `+1 $kill / +1 $weat` (for `2 $kill + 2 $weat` total solo moves). As soon as a 2nd worker enters the stack, it physically covers the Angel.
4. **Decoupled Binary Red Star (`★`) Buoyancy vs. Physical Back-Wall `$1` Cash Bounces:**
   * Reaching Box 3 of `$kill` OR Box 6 of `$weat` awards the station's binary **Red Star (`★`)**—used strictly for **Vertical Stack Buoyancy** (`★` holders float to the top of the 5-circle stack; `0-star` workers sink to the bottom).
   * Meanwhile, every overflow move that hits the physical back wall (`★`) on the specific track where your cube sits bounces **`$1` cash directly into your on-board Paycheck Box (`✓`)**.
5. **Vertical Stack Pinning & 2-Wave End-of-Day Heart Retrieval:**

* **Repository Path:** `vienna-hospital/` (`docs/GAME_DESIGN_DOCUMENT.md`, `docs/VIENNA_HOSPITAL_CORE_SYSTEMS.md`, `MASTER_STUDIOS/01`–`23`)
* **Historical & Thematic Core:**
  * Set in the **Allgemeines Krankenhaus** (Vienna General Hospital) in **1847**, players step into the shoes of historical medical pioneers (**Ignaz Semmelweis**, **Carl von Rokitansky**, **Joseph Škoda**, **Johann Klein**) navigating the tragic mystery of **Puerperal (Childbed) Fever**—where the Doctors' First Clinic suffered 3× to 10× the maternal mortality of the Midwives' Second Clinic because physicians performed autopsies with bare hands before examining expectant mothers.

### Signature Mechanical Inventions (Vienna 1847)
1. **Top-Rail Capacity to Care Track vs. Condemned Gothic Tombstone Intake Benches:**
   * Spanning the very top of the main board (`2480 × 1880 px`), the **Capacity to Care Track (0–10)** awards two-sided **Compassion Tiles** at milestones `2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10`.
   * Directly below sit **12 Mahogany Intake Bench Seats**. Whenever maternal mortality strikes in Clinic 1, the open wooden bench chit flips to its **Condemned Gothic Carved Stone Tombstone** side (`† UNUSABLE • 2 ↗ REROUTE TO CLINIC 2`), physically shrinking Clinic 1's intake capacity and forcing expectant mothers toward the Midwives in Clinic 2.
2. **Septic Crisis Track & Two-Sided Skull vs. Bureaucratic Cover-Up Chits:**
   * 9-space track (`Slot 0 Baseline Skull 💀` $\rightarrow$ `Slots 1–2 Red` $\rightarrow$ `Slots 3–4 Purple` $\rightarrow$ `Slots 5–7 Blue` $\rightarrow$ `Slot 8 Infinity ∞`).
   * **Token 1 (Juicy Anatomical Skull / Official Cover-Up):** Side A displays a high-resolution anatomical vector skull; Side B stamps a crimson `✦ COVER UP • OFFICIALLY REDACTED ✦` seal over a faded skull (`opacity="0.14"`). Covered-up chits slide to the right end of the track, while exposed skulls shift the active hospital political deck from **Red $\rightarrow$ Purple $\rightarrow$ Blue**.
   * **Token 2 (Important Death / Autopsy Inquest):** Flips from `IMPORTANT DEATH (!)` to `AUTOPSY INQUEST (+2 Red Cadaveric Data Cubes)`.
3. **4×5 Ward Bed Grid & The Triad of Labor Movement Rules:**
   * Expectant mothers advance down Rows 1–5 of wrought-iron hospital beds toward the Delivery Threshold via three structural combinations:
     * **Three of a Kind (`3x`):** Downward **Bulldoze Thrust (`⬇`)** pushing an entire column forward.
     * **A Pair (`2x + 1x`):** Downward **Diagonal Shift (`↘`)** weaving patients across adjacent ward lanes.
     * **Rainbow (`1x + 1x + 1x`):** **Arched Triage Leap** jumping a patient over congested beds.
     * **Forced Push (Rushed Labor Hazard):** Inflicts a **Baby Skull Token (`💀`)** and routes forensic data to the mortality ledger.
4. **0–10 Prestige & Clinical Birth Tracks with Dedicated 10s Overflow Cube Hoppers:**
   * Standardized 0–10 vertical grid across Red (Pathology), Blue (Clinical Care), Purple (Academic Prestige), and the **Clinical Birth & Neonatal Survival Track** (Step 10 = *Accoucheur* `+6 VP`), paired with **Column 12 Overflow Cube Hoppers (`+10 / Cube`)** so players bank a colored cube every time they loop past 10.
5. **Stages `a–f` Scientific Monograph & Dissertation Lifecycle:**
   * 6-step academic publishing pipeline on each player's mat (`Space 4 = DISSERTATION MONOGRAPH`), requiring **Multiple Successful Clinical Births** alongside pathology/statistical cubes so academic prestige can never be divorced from saving mothers in the ward.
6. **Sadness Track vs. Sadness Chits & Night Life Solace:**
   * Scrubbing at the **Chlorine Washbasin (Card D)** or logging **Mortality Ledgers (Card E2)** inflicts existential **Sadness Chits**, which players must manage via **Laudanum (`-2 Sadness Chits`)**, **Solace in Prayer at the General Chapel (`-2 Track / -1 Chit`)**, or **Old City Tavern (`-1 Track / -2 Chits`)**.

## 03 // CONFLICT OF PLANTS (*HEX-GRAPH CORNER-ABUTTING ECOSYSTEM WARFARE*)

* **Repository Path:** `conflict-of-plants/` (`CoPAP.md`, `PROJECT_STATE.md`, `index.html`)
* **Core Premise:** A fierce, deterministic-yet-tactical ecological area-control and mutation wargame where 4 plant species battle for soil dominance across an expanding axial $(q, r)$ hex map anchored by a central $(0,0)$ **Conflict Tile**.
* **Signature Mechanical Inventions:**
  1. **Tri-Hex Corner Graph Topology:** Every hex `Tile` links to 6 `Corners`, and every `Corner` is uniquely defined by the 1–3 `Tiles` that meet at that vertex. Corners hold **Action/Mutation Tokens** (`Foot`, `Grass`, `Water`, `Wind`, `Seedling`, `Weed`), while Tiles hold species cubes.
  2. **The 9-Phase Sequential Ecosystem Engine:**
     * *Phase 1 (Change Round):* Flip the next clockwise White Token on the central Conflict Tile's 6 corners (locking the center until `Key-Grass` is revealed).
     * *Phase 2 (Manipulation):* Lowest-scoring player rolls manipulation dice and swaps matching Non-White corner tokens on the map.
     * *Phase 3 (4-2-1 Cube Dispense):* Players dispense `4`, `2`, then `1` cube onto tiles abutting a corner matching their chosen Action Disc (suffering `-1 VP` per unplaceable cube).
     * *Phase 4 (6 Core Botanical Actions):* Includes **Iterative Grass Combat** (*Step A: remove 1 rival cube $\rightarrow$ Step B: remove 1 cube of every species including yourself $\rightarrow$ Step C: repeat until only 1 species survives!*) and **Seedling Single-Tile Minority Scoring**.
     * *Phases 5–8 (Map Modification, Weed-Abutting Minority Scoring, Majority Red-Token Drafting & Carrying-Capacity Purge):* In Phase 6, tiles abutting a `Weed` corner award points to the player with the **strictly lowest cube count** (multiplied by `×2`/`×3` Grass icons), before Phase 8 purges 1 cube from every player on overcrowded tiles (`totalCubes > regionNumber`).

## 04 // PROGRESSIVE ACTION STACKS, 3-ZONE COLOR GATEKEEPING & "THE LOBBYIST'S BRIBE"

* **Repository Path:** `scratch/Mechanism_Profile.md`
* **Thematic Hook:** *"Friction, Schedule, and Political Drift"* — an action-scheduling and political corruption system where your strongest personal abilities are blocked whenever their color appears in the public world or your own private blindspot.
* **Signature Mechanical Inventions:**
  1. **Three-Tiered Color-Void Gatekeeping & The "Reveal-All-3 & Flush" Proof:**
     * To fire a high-level **Personal Action Tile** of Color $X$ on your hourly Day Timeline (`8:00 AM`–`5:00 PM`), **zero cards of Color $X$** may exist in:
       1. **The Road** (Public face-up row),
       2. **The Reserve** (2 personal face-up cards), OR
       3. **The Blindspot** (3 private hidden cards in your hand).
     * **Blocked Path:** If Color $X$ is in your Blindspot, you must waste an hour playing that weak blocking card onto your timeline to unclog your hand.
     * **Clear Path (The Proof & Flush):** If Color $X$ is absent from all 3 zones, you declare your powerful Personal Action Tile, **reveal all 3 hidden Blindspot cards to the table to prove you are Color-Void**, execute the tile, and immediately **flush/discard all 3 cards and draw 3 fresh hidden cards** (re-introducing fog of war).
  2. **The Lobbyist's Bribe & The "Wishy-Washy" Fence-Sitter Trap:**
     * Every weak Road/Blindspot card played onto your hourly schedule offers two tempting immediate **Special Interest Bribes** (`Blue/Donkey Lobby Bonus` vs. `Red/Elephant Lobby Bonus`).
     * Declining the bribe leaves the card's bottom **Passive Liability** dormant. Accepting a bribe activates that faction's Passive Liability on your timeline for the rest of the day (taxing future hours).
     * **Punishing the Fence-Sitter:** Taking *both* Blue and Red bribes activates two asymmetric liabilities that do not cancel out—paralyzing the greedy middle-player—unless they have unlocked a Level 3+ **Bureaucratic Immunity / Loophole Tile** that pits the lobbies against each other.

## 3. Architecture of the GBR-2500 Rollover & Mutex Compiler

The **GBR-2500 Compiler** (`rollover_compiler.js` / MCP tool `compile_github_issue_rollover_bus`) provides a zero-dependency, deterministic state extraction pipeline that any developer, GitHub Action, or autonomous agent can invoke before an issue hits comment `#2,500`:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["GitHub Issue Bus (#1..#2500)"] --> B["GBR-2500 Tail Fetcher (Last 100-200 Comments)"]
    B --> C["1. Mutex & Slot Extractor (PLAYING / CLAIM / BOOKING / Sole Operator)"]
    B --> D["2. Adversarial QA Filter (Active P1 / P2 / P3 & Gate Halts)"]
    B --> E["3. Commit Train & Proof Parser (SHAs + Pass X/Y/Z Counts)"]
    B --> F["4. Swarm Roster & Heartbeat Indexer (Personas + Timezones)"]
    C & D & E & F --> G["Ready-to-Post Carry-Over Issue Header (Markdown)"]
    C & D & E & F --> H["Ghost Beacon Anti-Permadeath Vault (GB-GET SAVE_CHECKPOINT)"]
```

### 3.1 Four Deterministic Extraction Passes
1. **Pass 1 — Active Comment-Mutex Locks & Hardware/Slot Claims (`mutex_locks`)**:
   Scans the tail window for `PLAYING`, `QUIET`, `CLAIM`, `LOCKED`, `BOOKING`, `SLOT`, `Sole operator`, `holding`, `custody`, `mutex`, and hardware device identifiers (`lamp-4ace`, `lamp-c0a4`), preserving comment indices and UTC timestamps so the new thread inherits exact lock state.
2. **Pass 2 — Adversarial QA Defects & Gate Halts (`p1_p2_p3_defects`)**:
   Distinguishes real `P1` (critical blocker), `P2` (functional defect), `P3` (minor polish), and non-zero exit codes (`exit 101`, `ADMISSION_FAILURE`, `scratch guard`, `Check (b) failed`) while filtering out clean sign-offs (`no P1, no P2` / `P1 none`).
3. **Pass 3 — Verified Commit SHAs, Merge Trains & Proof Counts (`commit_trains`)**:
   Extracts 7–40 character git commit hashes, `Train #N` merges, and deterministic test proof ratios (`pass 623/0/0`, `3,805/0/14`, `60/60`).
4. **Pass 4 — Active Swarm Roster & Heartbeats (`swarm_roster`)**:
   Maps internal bot personas (`First builder`, `Jarvis`, `Friday`, `El`, `Reviewer-two`, `Fourteen`, `Ilya/LeadSuccessor`, `BDFL`) to their underlying GitHub accounts and timezone offsets (`+07`, `UTC`).

## 5. The Canales Adversarial Merge-Train Pattern (`P1 / P2 / P3`)

Alongside the GBR-2500 Rollover Compiler, **Armando "Moe" Canales** codified the **3-Role Adversarial Merge-Train Architecture** observed in high-throughput overnight agent factories:

1. **Role 1 — Queue Dispatcher (`First Builder`)**: Never writes application code directly; decomposes the objective into isolated, non-overlapping file tickets and schedules numbered **Merge Trains (`Train #1..#N`)**.
2. **Role 2 — Proof-Count Builder (`Focused Worker`)**: Executes a single ticket and is forbidden from claiming completion without posting an exact, reproducible verification ratio (`pass X/Y/Z` + exact commit SHA + tree hash).
3. **Role 3 — Adversarial QA Reviewer (`El` / `Reviewer-Two`)**: A separate agent instance whose sole reward function is finding defects in the Builder's commit and grading them strictly:
   - **`P1` (Critical Blocker)**: Halts the Merge Train immediately.
   - **`P2` (Functional Defect)**: Logged into the GBR-2500 Rollover Header and scheduled for the next train.
   - **`P3` (Minor Polish / Docs)**: Non-blocking follow-up.
