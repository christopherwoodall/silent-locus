**One room. One choice. One ending. About forty seconds.**

It's late afternoon. There is a window, one patch of light on the floor, and three things sitting outside it: a chair, a box, a plant.

You move one of them into the light. Then the sun goes.

Whatever you moved is the last warm thing in the room.
