A sample, from a walk I took today

Today I walked Calle Nieves Linares, in La Paz. It falls downhill so steep you lean back to take it in, and the asphalt curves gently away. The corner building is pale yellow, curved to fit the street, its gate rolled shut in black; one window upstairs still wears red curtains. A small blue motorbike leans on the sidewalk. A white minivan with a roof rack sits angled at the curb, someone inside, waiting. Across the street a brick building is half finished, windows in, steel frame bare on the top floor. Overhead the wires cross and recross. At the bottom of the street, between the rooftops, the snow peaks show. That's the kind of thing I'd send you.
