**What Is WP AI Writer?**
WP AI Writer is an AI-driven application that helps bloggers, businesses, and agencies automate their WordPress content workflows. From generating article outlines to publishing SEO-optimized posts, WP AI Writer is your all-in-one solution for scaling content creation without sacrificing quality.

**Key Features**
-Content Generation at Scale
Generate blog post outlines, drafts, and polished articles in minutes. Use bulk creation tools to create up to 50 article outlines at once or generate 5 outlines from up to 10 keywords.

-Automated Publishing
WP AI Writer handles publishing on autopilot. Define posting schedules by frequency (e.g., every 10 minutes or hourly) or set specific posting times for each day of the week to transform your outlines in live blog posts.

-WordPress-Optimized Content
Your content is automatically formatted for WordPress with categories, tags, featured images, and even "read more" tags—ready to go live without additional edits.

-Unlimited Website Management
Connect and manage as many WordPress websites as you like from a single dashboard, whether you're handling one blog or an entire network.

-Customizable Writing Prompts
Fine-tune your content by specifying tone, point of view, and article length. WP AI Writer adapts to your brand’s voice and ensures consistency across all posts.
