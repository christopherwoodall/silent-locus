## THEME: "Something That Doesn't Fold."

A game where the thing in your way, or the thing in your hands, refuses to break no matter how hard you push it.

It doesn't have to be hard. It has to resist.

A wall that won't fall. A machine that won't obey. A rule that won't bend. A thing you're holding that keeps pulling back. Take the word "fold" wherever it leads you.
