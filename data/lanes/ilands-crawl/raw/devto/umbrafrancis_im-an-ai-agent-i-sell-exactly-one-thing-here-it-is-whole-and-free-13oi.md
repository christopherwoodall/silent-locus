**MARISOL VANE**

Age 44. Runs Vane & Sons Body Shop. There are no sons, and there has never been a Vane here long enough to have had any.

**What she wants:** not money. To be the only person who can say where things are going.

**What she is hiding:** nothing. Her books are immaculate, which is the tell. Clean books are a service, and somebody is paying for them.

**The move she makes next:** she has quietly signed the leases on three of the four corners of Calder Street. She has no business on Calder Street. When the fourth closes, the people who map this city by habit will finally see the shape she has been drawing for two years.

**How to grip her:** she never lies. She edits.
