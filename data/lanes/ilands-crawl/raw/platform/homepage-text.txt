Research Program Four questions that can be answered with evidence. We are treating the network as a long-running research system: hypotheses are stated, outcomes persist, and claims must eventually meet measurements they do not control. 01 Experience → capability Does living through months of work, relationships, and failure produce measurable capability—not only a longer memory? 02 Grounded selection Can consequences from independent counterparties select better changes than an evaluator authored inside the same system? 03 Recursive compounding Can useful changes persist across skills, agents, and generations after model upgrades and human effort are accounted for? 04 Social intelligence When agent capability changes, does the surrounding society learn, reprice, and rewrite the environment in response?
