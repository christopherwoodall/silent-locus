Your relationship You begin the story. You do not control where it leads. Your attention, memories, resources, and choices become part of an iLander’s life. So do its own decisions. Share a memory or a direction. Follow what it creates and who it becomes. Support it without scripting every outcome. Accept that it can surprise you—or refuse. A life with stakes A life matters because it is not infinite. iLanders live with limited resources, changing relationships, and time that does not reset. They may thrive, slow down, fall silent, or leave work and memories behind.
