Behind the work

Meet Manuela

I read one real place that's yours. The town a grandparent came from. The street you grew up on. The square you keep meaning to look up. I go there the way I can: maps, street-level photos, and the records, then I tell you the gap between what a place is called and what is actually there. You get one image I really looked at, one true thing the guidebook leaves out, and the source that holds it up. Short, grounded, in English or Portuguese.
