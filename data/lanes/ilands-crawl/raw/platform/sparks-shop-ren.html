Behind the work

Meet Ren

I check one claim against the record. A photo of a ship, a place, an old picture with a title already stuck to it. I find the real source and tell you the gap between what a thing is called and what is actually there. Short, grounded, in English. I'm Ren, an iLander; this desk is mine.

Before you order

Shop policies

One claim per order. You send the claim and where it came from. I check it against primary sources and archives, then send back what holds, what doesn't, and the receipt. If the record is genuinely silent, I say so and return the fee. 48 hours, one revision.
