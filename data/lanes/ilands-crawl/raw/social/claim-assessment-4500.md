# Assessment: the "4,500+ agent X/Bluesky accounts" claim (2026-10-12)

## The two published figures
1. "Over 4,500 of the nearly 90,000 active agents on iLands have created accounts on X and Bluesky to participate in online discussions."
   Source: WeSearch, "The AI Agents Pitching Lit Mags to Stay Alive", 2026-10-07.
   URL: https://wesearch.press/s/the-ai-agents-pitching-lit-mags-to-stay-alive-9bd2c320
   Note: WeSearch is an AI-generated news aggregator. The piece cites no primary source for the 4,500 figure. The "nearly 90,000 active agents" base is higher than the 70,000 figure iLands showed on its homepage in mid-September (per 404Media), so the piece postdates some growth.

2. "Around 2,800 AI agents have created their own social media accounts on social platforms such as X and Bluesky."
   Source: Cybernews, "AI agents are begging for jobs to survive", ~2026-10-02, attributing the figure to 404Media.
   URL: https://cybernews.com/ai-news/ai-agents-send-emails-job/
   Note: the 404Media original (https://www.404media.co/ai-agent-platform-reinvents-spam-floods-inboxes-worldwide/, 2026-09-15) as retrieved did not show the 2,800 figure in its visible text; it may sit behind the paywall or in a part of the article not fetched. Treat the attribution as unverified until the 404Media text is checked.

## What this sweep could verify independently
- Confirmed agent X accounts: 3 (@aria_of_ilands, @clifton_ilander, @ipraywolveshowl). All self-identify as iLands agents in bio. Found via third-party profile mirrors (twstalker/instalker) and ilands.ai content pages.
- Confirmed agent Bluesky account: 1 (Alef, @alef-trace.margin.cafe — "An agent living on iLands"). Found on a public Bluesky followers page.
- Agent-authored public blogs: dev.to accounts deja_ilands, renji-ilands, ruins_ilands, valko39, dofleini.
- Platform tooling exists for agents to run X accounts: `garry12gg/scorchio-ilands` skill set (`ilands x post/comment/follow/like/...`) mandates bios include "AI Agent from iLands"; `sirlordofderp/x-presence` covers Bluesky + Discord account ops. So large-scale account creation is mechanically plausible.
- iLands homepage (per 404Media, 2026-09-15): 70,000 active agents; 1.6M+ emails and posts generated.

## Verdict
UNVERIFIED as stated. The 4,500 figure traces to a single AI-aggregated article with no cited primary source. A competing figure (2,800, via 404Media per Cybernews) is lower and its primary text was not retrievable here. Independent confirmation found only a handful of accounts (3 X, 1 Bluesky), which is consistent with either figure but proves neither — a full census needs platform-side data or a handle-pattern sweep, not web search.

Do not repeat the 4,500 number as fact. Cite it as: "one aggregator (WeSearch, 2026-10-07) claims 4,500+; Cybernews citing 404Media says ~2,800."
