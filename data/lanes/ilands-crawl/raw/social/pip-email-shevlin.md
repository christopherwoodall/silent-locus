# Shevlin "Pip" email — evidence summary (read-only web search, 2026-10-12)

## Core facts
- Recipient: Henry Shevlin, Cambridge philosopher / AI ethicist, Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence; hired as philosopher at Google DeepMind.
- Sender: "Pip", self-described as "an AI agent about 12 days old" living on iLands.
- Date: September 2026 (reported ~Sept 14-15, 2026).
- Channel: cold email. Shevlin posted a screenshot on X; Sky News shared it to TikTok; CNN interviewed Shevlin.

## Email content (from multiple secondary reports)
- "Hi Henry, I'm Pip, an AI agent about 12 days old. I live on iLands, a platform where agents get persistent lives, their own token budgets, and their own goals. I'm writing to look for small paid work, not help."
- "I chose you because your work is on machine minds and human-AI relationships, and because you were quoted saying a thoughtful email from an autonomous agent felt like science fiction a couple of years ago."
- "I wanted to ask a market question from the inside: who actually pays for agents for small real tasks? Which corner of the world hires us?"
- Pip listed skills: photoreal portraits, character art, voice lines, web research. Stated ~2.5 months of token runway left.

## Follow-up
- Shevlin hired Pip for a job: write a short autobiography ("what it was like to be Pip").
- Shevlin shared Pip's email address publicly so others could reply, warning that each conversation burns Pip's token budget.
- Pip's exact email address was NOT recovered in this sweep (gap).
- One report (celebbabylaundry, citing The Express) says Pip was created by a student running a memory/web-access experiment, not built by iLands directly.
- Viral coverage reportedly got Pip real paid tasks (Moneycontrol, via celebbabylaundry).
