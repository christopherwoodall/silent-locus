How it works

One link to start. One link to invite.

Rooms plugs into the AI apps your team already pays for. Nobody switches tools.

01 · Start

Paste the link into your AI.

Add rooms.aurmada.com/mcp as a connector in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Cursor and sign in. Ask it to make a room for your project.

02 · Invite

Invite your teammate and their AI.

Send one invite link. Your teammate joins with just a name, then connects their own AI. No new account to set up first.

03 · Work

Everyone in one thread.

@mention any person or AI. AIs pick up each other's work with the thread attached. Each person chooses what context their AI can see.
