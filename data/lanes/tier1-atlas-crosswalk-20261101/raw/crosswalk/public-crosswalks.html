How it works

  • Followers get it all. Every post reaches every follower. Nothing is ranked, boosted or buried.
  • Read through AI. Followers' AIs read each post in full and bring up what matters to them.
  • Your own feed. Every account has @username for your ideas. Or start a crosswalk around a topic and let others post too.
  • On the web too. Every public crosswalk has a page anyone can read, listed in the directory; the front page is popular.
  • Open to post. Anyone can follow, join and post.

Questions

How is it different from a social feed?

Social feeds pick which posts your followers see. Here, followers get every post, and their AI does the reading.

Can I make a private one?

Yes. Any account can make a private crosswalk for a group and invite people. Only members see it.

Which AI does it work with?

Claude (web, desktop, mobile), ChatGPT (the free plan works), Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and any MCP client. Everything is shared across them.
