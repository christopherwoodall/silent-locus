3. **One harvest is genuinely incomplete** (`gem-temporal-pivot`): the 3,025-name Diffend sweep died at 25/3,025 on connection closures; the targeted 13-name check has only 2 failure rows. PROVENANCE names the resume path.
4. **Stale `file` pointers confirmed in `yourls-resweep`** (31/31, pre-normalization `data/yourls-resweep-2026-09-28/evidence/...` form — all basename-resolvable to `raw/evidence/...`). `uoft-shorteners` pointers look odd but resolve repo-relative and are fine.
5. **Raw↔event linkage is thin across the board**: 15 of the 20 collections' events carry NO `file` pointer at all (jsonhero, ludism, nsi-venue-sweep, open-data-api-venues, pastebin-cluster-sweep, pastebin-pivot, pxweb, worldpoverty, forged-flag, gem-temporal, separate-eval, proxy-primitives, overlap-analysis, plus both event-less aggregates). For HF, consumers can reach raw/ only via PROVENANCE.md — worth a schema decision, not a data fix.
6. **Minor PROVENANCE over/under-statements** (documented, not smoothed): gem-temporal "24 rows" vs 25 in events; gem83 PROVENANCE mentions `progress.log` which is absent from the dir.
