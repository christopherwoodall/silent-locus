## 4. What the numbers say (statistician's verdict: the gap dissolves)

1. **The arithmetic refutes the thesis.** At DE/FR's ~5–8% world AI share and ~7–8 observed agent operations globally, λ ≈ 0.4–0.6 and P(observing 0) ≈ **0.55–0.67**. Zero is the modal outcome, not an anomaly.
2. **Wrong denominator.** Observed swarms are generated by a rare, lab-concentrated process (frontier agent-eval escapes + state-scale collection fleets — ~2 entities worldwide). DE/FR field zero entities in that class: Mistral is a model/API company, not a public-surface eval operator; Aleph Alpha is B2B; Helsing is classified defense. Correct denominator → expected ≈ 0 → observed 0 is exactly predicted.
3. **English-default tooling blinds the detector.** DseWiki precedent: agents wrote English on a German-locale site. A DE/FR-operated swarm doing generic English web tasks is linguistically invisible to our pipeline by construction.

Right as a prior, wrong as a posterior.
