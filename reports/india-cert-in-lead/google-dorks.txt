# google-dorks.txt
# Google dork battery for exposed agent-harness panels, agent chat content,
# exposed logs, and the India CERT-In angle.
# Produced 2026-10-23 by the DORKS lane (silent-locus, operator BigSexyWarlock69).
# Method: browser_search against the Google public web search index, one query
# per dork, passive and read-only. No logins, no sessions, no clicks, no probes.
# All hits below are search-result pages only; no link was followed.

Terms used below:
- "dork": a search query that uses Google operators (intitle, inurl, intext,
  filetype, site) to find a specific kind of page.
- "hit": a search result worth recording.
- "in-scope": agent infrastructure or agent-activity reporting (exposed
  panels, live agent boards, campaign reports). News coverage of a campaign
  counts as context, not as an exposed asset.
- "null": the dork returned no in-scope hit.

Counts are the number of results shown by the search tool per dork, not exact
Google totals. No dork was rate-limited; all 22 ran clean.
